# Week 06 — MRI Image Formation: Quiz Solutions

Worked solutions to the quiz on **Magnetic Resonance Image Formation**, tying
together the Week 05 MR-physics concepts (precession, FID, Bloch equations,
relaxation, Larmor/slice selection, and Damadian's tumor-detection paper).

## Contents

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [MRI_Quiz_Solutions.ipynb](MRI_Quiz_Solutions.ipynb) | Written answers to all 7 quiz questions, each with illustrative simulations: FID generation (Faraday induction), FID decay scenarios, $M_x/M_y/M_z$ dynamics, the Larmor/slice-selection relation, and Damadian's tumor-vs-normal relaxation times. |

## Topics covered

- **Q1** Proton (spin + magnetic moment, related by $\gamma$) vs an iron filing; precession
- **Q2** Faraday induction in RF coils; origin of the Free Induction Decay (FID)
- **Q3** Why FID decays (dephasing) and when it would not ($T_2\to\infty$)
- **Q4** Transverse $M_x, M_y$ (90° out of phase) measured for imaging; $T_1$ recovery
- **Q5** The FID solves the **Bloch equations**; $T_2$ governs transverse decay
- **Q6** **Larmor equation** $\omega_0=\gamma B_0$ and its use in gradient slice selection
- **Q7** Damadian (1971): elevated $T_1$ (spin–lattice) and $T_2$ (spin–spin) in tumor tissue
